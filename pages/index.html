Physics Notes

This website provides comprehensive notes for Pearson Edexcel International AS and A Level Physics: every topic from Unit 1 to Unit 5, plus the practical skills for Units 3 and 6. Each topic has thorough notes paired with diagrams you can actually play with, and every important equation is derived step by step so you understand where it comes from instead of just memorising it.

Physics is not about memorising, but about understanding.

How to use these notes

Interactive

Diagrams with sliders, buttons and things you can drag. Change one quantity and watch what happens to the others.

Derivation

Step-by-step derivations. Press Next step and try to predict the next line before you reveal it.

Beyond the spec

Orange boxes go a little past the syllabus to explain why something works. They are clearly marked as not part of the Edexcel spec, so you won't be examined on them.

Formula sheet

The equation is printed on the list of formulae you get in the exam, so you need to know how to use it, not memorise it.
